Chapter 25 — Deeper Than Darnassus

Rambleon's long walk through Ashenvale found its edges that night. Cassidy met him again near Silverwing Grove, the ease between them needing no words after so many nights, and a rogue named Nebben fell in for a short while at Bloodtooth Camp before peeling off his own way. The two of them cleared Raene's Cleansing among the Bloodtooth orcs, then struck south to the Zoram Strand, where the ground opened beneath them into Blackfathom Deeps — a druid named Dyl and a shaman called Boozebraid joining the push, and soon after, another druid, Andy. Down in that drowned dark the five of them went from chamber to chamber — the Drowned Sacellum, the Pool of Ask'ar, the Moonshrine, the Forgotten Pool — Ghamoo-ra and Lady Sarevess falling, then old Serra'kis, then Twilight Lord Kelris himself, and at the bottom of it all, Aku'mai. A rare blade or two came up out of that silt, worth the carrying.

They surfaced into Darnassus, and Rambleon lingered there a while, trading and wandering the terraces before Cassidy led him out again — through Rut'theran Village, across the Veiled Sea, back to Darkshore and down into Ashenvale once more. Andy, Dyl, and Boozebraid parted ways at the top of the city steps; the company had thinned to the two who always seemed to find their way back to each other. Nightsong Woods gave up an old bear and a pair of Foulweald raiders, and then came the Dor'Danil Barrow Den, where Taneel Darkwood and Uthil Mooncall both went down to Insane Druids. The Barrow Den was not kind to him that night — he died there once, and again, and again, each time the walk back from the graveyard a little longer than the last, until the fourth attempt finally held.

Ashenvale closed warmly enough after that. Mystral Lake, Silverwind Refuge, and the road back to Astranaar, where thirty years of life settled onto Rambleon's shoulders at last — Dartol's Rod of Transformation in hand, a silver ring and a blade besides, and a quiet, private satisfaction in the number alone. He took his picture there before Cassidy parted from him outside Southshore sometime past one, the thread between them loosening again for the night.

Alone again, he ranged wide and strange — Southshore, Menethil Bay, Ironforge's forge-light, the Great Forge's heat, Searing Gorge's ash, Burning Steppes, Elwynn's green, and finally Stormwind's Trade District, where a hunter named Atoh, then a mage and priest pair, Postpartum and Faelen, then Karn and Dro drifted briefly through his company and out again, each a nod and nothing more. He ended the night back in Southshore, contract delivered, the Baron's business now his to finish another time.
