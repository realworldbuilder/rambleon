Chapter 20 — The Cursed Crew of the Lost Fleet

Rambleon Birdsong · October 11, 2026 · 4h 25m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney

Rambleon found Silverwind Refuge much as he'd left it, and there the summoner's errand that had followed him out of Nightsong Woods and Satyrnaar at last came to its quiet end — one more small debt paid toward Raene's long cleansing, which by now felt almost like an old companion of its own. From there the road ran its familiar course: the lamplight of the Lion's Pride Inn, then down into the Wetlands, ground he'd walked enough now to call known.

The marsh kept him busy. Fen Creepers thinned from the Sundown reeds twelve at a time, a Phytoblade turned up worth the keeping, and the business of Dun Modr closed out clean before the road pointed him somewhere new — across Thandol Span and into Arathi Highlands for the first time, Stromgarde's walls rising ahead through unfamiliar hills. The walk back from the graveyard found him four times before the night was through, once on that span, once in the highlands, twice more later in Saltspray Glen — the record keeps no more of it than that. But Stromgarde Keep gave him something to set against it: level twenty-seven, quiet and earned, worth a picture before he turned back for the coast.

At the Lost Fleet the cursed sailors kept him company longer than most living ones did that night. Shadeborn, a priest, and Verru, a hunter, fell in with him a few minutes among the wrecks; after they'd gone their own ways, Morrigan, a warrior, and Melindhra, a mage, joined him long enough to finish what that doomed crew's sailors and marines had left undone. Four strangers, none of them lingering, but each made the wreck-haunted shallows feel less lonely for the while they shared it. A quick errand to Ironforge and a loop through unfamiliar corners of Dun Morogh — Misty Pine Refuge, Amberstill Ranch — bracketed the work neatly before he returned to it.

Saltspray Glen took the rest of the night: razormaws culled for their eggs, their incisors counted out one by one, until Trying Times and the needling both were done. He finished at Angerfang Encampment, closing out Nek'rosh's Gambit with Nek'rosh himself still waiting to be dealt with, and came back, as he always seemed to, to the Lion's Pride Inn to let the fire have the last word.
