Chapter 12 — A Quiet Standing in the Trade District

Rambleon Birdsong · October 3, 2026 · 40m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney
← Chapter 11 — Into the Deadmines

Rambleon logged in to find himself already where Cassidy had left him the night before — the Trade District, Stormwind's lamplight unchanged, the stone underfoot as familiar as a well-worn path through Ashenvale. There was no errand waiting, no quest-giver's nod to answer, only the city going about its business around him the way it always did, indifferent and alive. He stood a while and let it be enough.

Forty minutes passed like that, unhurried. He did not go looking for Westfall's dust or the Deadmines' damp again, did not seek out Cassidy or any of the others who had crowded so companionably around him the night before. It was a night for standing still, for letting the ache of all that walking settle out of his legs. Near the end he paused long enough to mark the moment properly and let a picture be taken, a small thing to set beside the memory of Gilnid's fall and his own quiet climb to twenty-one.

No one joined him. No blade was drawn. He thought, briefly, of the mines still damp in memory, of Cassidy's steady company these eight nights running, and found he did not mind the solitude — a hunter learns, eventually, that not every night in Stormwind need be spent in motion. Then he logged out, the Trade District no quieter for his leaving than it had been for his staying.
