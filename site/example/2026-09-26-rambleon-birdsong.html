Chapter 6 — Ameth'Aran's Reckoning

Rambleon Birdsong · September 26, 2026 · 3h 20m in Azeroth
StoryRecapJourney
← Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore

The ruins of Ameth'Aran had more to give than the night before had let him finish, and Rambleon returned to them with the same patience Ban'ethil Hollow had taught him. Cursed and Wailing and Writhing Highborne fell in turn among the broken columns until the last of the Highborne relics was in hand — seven of seven — and with them came his fifteenth year, quiet as fourteen had been, a small satisfaction he carried with him as he read what remained of the Lay and the Fall of Ameth'Aran among the stones that had once been a city. The reading closed something Corithras's errands had opened, and he moved on lighter for it.

Auberdine gave him fresh business before the day was old — a bounty on a murloc called Murkdeep, fruit to gather from the sea, a prospector gone missing somewhere the notes didn't say — and then, past midday, Cassidy found him again, familiar as she had been through the Barrow Den and Darkshore both. Together they cut north to the Tower of Althalaxx, where Delmanis the Hated went down and Balthule's letter was won twice over, the first telling of it undone by a death in the tower itself before the second stuck. A sample drawn from the Cliffspring River closed one thread even as Balthule's letter opened another, bound now for Ashenvale and a purifier named Delgren, though that road would wait for another night. A brief crossing of the Veiled Sea brought him back to Teldrassil and into Darnassus proper, pictures taken at both thresholds, a courier's errand to Nessa finished before he turned south again.

Mist's Edge and Twilight Shore, when he reached them, remembered him from the night before, and he them — the ground there simply did not let a hunter walk it without paying something first. He went down again and again among the Greymist, the dying almost routine by now, a shrug and a walk back in rather than any real fear of it. A Paladin named Caerwyn and a fellow Hunter, Reighna, fell in with him just long enough to see Murkdeep finally slain and the bounty claimed, then went their own ways as strangers on the road do. Later a second Paladin, Elentria, kept him company for a shorter stretch still, gone almost as soon as she'd arrived. Sea creature bones and a strangely marked box, gathered from the wrack of Twilight Shore, went at last to Gwennyth in Auberdine, the coastline's small debts finally settled. Cassidy parted from him as the afternoon wore toward evening, and Rambleon logged off with Darkshore's tides still working at the rocks behind him.
