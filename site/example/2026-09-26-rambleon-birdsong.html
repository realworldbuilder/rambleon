Chapter 6 — The Tides of Twilight Shore

Rambleon Birdsong · September 26, 2026 · 6h 44m in Azeroth
StoryRecapJourney
← Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore

Rambleon began the day back among the fallen columns of Ameth'Aran, finishing what the ruins still owed him — the Lay of Ameth'Aran read, its darker counterpart read after, the tale of the highborne's fall closed between one page and the next. The tools he'd gathered there found their purpose complete, and word came quiet as such things do: his fifteenth year of adventuring, no fanfare needed for a number that had settled into its own rhythm. Auberdine handed him fresh errands before noon was done — a murloc chieftain wanted, fruit of the sea, a prospector gone missing — and he set out to find them.

Cassidy found him again past midday, as she so often did now, and the two picked their way toward the Tower of Althalaxx, where old parchments waited to be read and old business waited to be finished twice over — its errand completed once, then handed back to him the moment it was done. The tower was not kind to him; he died there, twice, and rose both times the way Ban'ethil Hollow and Mist's Edge had already taught his body to expect. A detour carried him briefly home — across the Veiled Sea to Rut'theran Village, a word delivered in Darnassus, a picture kept of the crossing — before Darkshore called him back.

Twilight Shore proved worse than the tower. He went under there again and again, the tide and its Greymist folk taking him down more times than he cared to count, until dying in that surf became simply the shape of the afternoon, shrugged off each time as before. A paladin named Caerwyn and a fellow hunter named Reighna fell in just long enough to help him run down the murloc Murkdeep, gone again within minutes; a paladin named Elentria kept him company for one turn of the tide alone. Cassidy stayed through the worst of it before parting ways as evening came on.

He returned past dusk to finish what Darkshore still asked of him. Sixteen came to him quietly in Bashal'Aran, between one thistle bear and the next, and a hunter named Softtaco walked with him briefly through a Den Mother's undoing. Later a druid named Menos, new to him, kept him company through the long climb back into Ameth'Aran's ghosts, past Jai'vhanel and Anaya Dawnrunner, to the ancient flame where Bashal'Aran's seal was finally broken. Seventeen found him in Twilight Vale not long after, and the Excavation's golems and gravelflint kept him busy near midnight. Cassidy rejoined him just as the night closed, familiar as ever, the two of them turning together into whatever waited next.
