Chapter 19 — The Long Circuit

Rambleon left the moonlight of Astranaar and went back to Raene's unfinished business, deeper into Nightsong Woods and down into Satyrnaar, where a second Highborne soul waited to be freed from the Tower of Althalaxx's old grip. Kayneth Stillwind's errand closed out at Forest Song, and from there came Vengeful Trail and Shared Fury, one quest opening into the next the way the forest paths did, until the Dor'Danil Barrow Den gave up an iron pommel for Raene's cleansing and the moonwell outside Astranaar took the offering once more. He walked back from the graveyard more than once across those woods, and once from the Tower of Althalaxx itself in Darkshore — the record keeps no more of it than that.

He was back in Astranaar when the moment he'd been working toward finally came: level twenty-six, a small bright thing he paused to mark with a picture before the Replica Crescent Necklace finished what the first attempt at it hadn't. The road after that ran north through Maestra's Post and down to Darkshore, where Auberdine's lamps were a welcome, familiar sight after a day on uneven ground. There he crossed paths with three strangers in quick succession — Mitzvah, a paladin, for a short stretch; then Trogwar and Jina, both druids, barely longer — the kind of brief company the road offers and takes back just as easily.

Auberdine held him for hours afterward, long enough to earn a quiet mark for time spent camped there, the fire doing what fires do for a hunter far from home. When he moved again it was back into Ashenvale proper, to Silverwind Refuge and the waters of Mystral Lake, where Raene's cleansing kept circling back to him like a chore that wouldn't finish, and the Elemental Bracers and Mage Summoner quests sent him as far as Dreadmist Peak, where Sarilus Foulborne's head went into his pack for proof of the job done.

What followed was less a quest than a restlessness — Rambleon crossing the Barrens' long gold roads, down through Thousand Needles and a glimpse of Feralas, out along Dustwallow's shore, through Mulgore and Shen'dralas and Desolace, over Stonetalon and back across the sea to Darkshore, a picture taken at nearly every threshold as though he meant to prove to himself how far the world went. By the time Astranaar's lanterns came back into view, the night had worn itself thin around him, and he let the road finally stop.
