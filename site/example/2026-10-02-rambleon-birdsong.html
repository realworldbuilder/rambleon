Chapter 11 — The Deeper Roads of Westfall

Rambleon Birdsong · October 2, 2026 · 3h 56m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney

Rambleon left Astranaar with the ease of long habit, Ashenvale falling away into Darkshore's familiar coast — worth a picture as the water opened before him — and then, for reasons his feet seemed to know better than his head, further still: across the Veiled Sea to Stormwind Harbor, through the Canals and the Trade District, and out again into Elwynn Forest, where he paused at Mirror Lake long enough to mark the moment and let the camera catch it. By the time Westfall's road brought him to Sentinel Hill, he had crossed half a continent without meaning to go anywhere in particular.

Cassidy caught up with him there, as she so often did now — the eighth night the two of them had shared since Aldrassil, a companionship that no longer needed finding its footing. Together they worked the Dust Plains and Moonbrook, down through Demont's Place and the Dagger Hills, Defias pillagers and knuckledusters falling before them in turn, and somewhere in that stretch he took on word of a dwarf called Thunderbrew, worth looking into when the chance came. A mage named Cortonna fell in with them for the briefest moment, gone again almost before he'd learned her name.

The Deadmines swallowed the rest of the night. Dire, Jstermy, and Emchetyre joined Cassidy and him as they pushed down through the Defias Hideout and the Mast Room, the enemies there thick — diggers, conjurers, evokers — with Rhahk'Zor falling to the company's combined effort and Sneed and his shredder not far behind. Rambleon died once in the Mast Room and again later in Ironclad Cove, walking back from Sentinel Hill's graveyard both times to find the others waiting. Between those two returns came Gnoam Sprecklesprocket's thanks for an underground assault, a goblin foundry's craftsmen and engineers cleared out, Gilnid faced and felled, and then, quietly, the turn of experience that carried him to level twenty-one — a small, private satisfaction amid all that digging and smoke.

After Ironclad Cove, Jstermy, Dire, and Emchetyre went their own ways, but Cassidy stayed on, and the two of them closed out the Dead Acre's harvest reapers and fleshrippers together. The long ride home took him through Stormwind once more, to the Dwarven District this time, where Underground Assault was finally laid to rest, a pair of uncommon armbands found their way into his pack, and a new errand — Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators — into his pocket for another night. Back at Sentinel Hill, as the night wound down, a warlock named Raven fell in beside him just before the lamps dimmed.
