Chapter 29 — Under the Mountain

Rambleon Birdsong · October 20, 2026 · 1h 03m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney
← Chapter 28 — The Circuit

Rambleon left Tinker Town with a fuller camp than he'd had in days. Cassidy fell in beside him first, familiar as ever after so many roads walked together, and close behind came new faces — Wes, Maru, Banished, Natalya, a priest or two and a pair of rogues gathering around him the way company sometimes does in Ironforge's forges and corridors. Maru peeled off again almost at once, barely long enough to learn his name, but the rest held together as they rode the tram, crossed back through the Military Ward, and came out under the open sky at the Gates of Ironforge.

Dun Morogh again — the gates, the frost-rimed road, Iceflow Lake catching what light there was. He paused there long enough for a picture before the group doubled back through Ironforge once more, as if testing the road before committing to it, and then struck out a second time, this time further, past the lake and down into the tunnels where Gnomeregan waited. He let one small thing go at the threshold, the Ink Supplies errand, loose thread cut before descending, and did not look back at it.

Gnomeregan swallowed the whole back half of the night. The Train Depot, the Clockwerk Run, the Hall of Gears, passage after passage of gnomish stone and machinery, the party moving through it together — Cassidy steady at his side as always, Wes and Natalya and Banished holding their ground beside him through all of it. Somewhere in that tangle of tunnels they brought down Grubbis, and Rambleon felt the small, solid satisfaction of a thing finally finished, even if the ledger showed no new quest closed for it. The Hall of Gears saw them twice more before the night began to loosen its grip — Wes gone, then Natalya, then Banished, the company thinning the way it does when an hour grows late and people have other fires to get back to.

He left Gnomeregan alone in the end, the tunnels giving way to the familiar cobbles of Stormwind's Trade District, and took one last picture there, a bookend to the one he'd taken descending into Dun Morogh. No ground gained in level, no quest closed for the ledger, but a mountain's worth of stone had passed beneath his boots, and five companions, old and new, had shared some part of that road with him.
