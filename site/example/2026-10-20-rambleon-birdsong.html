Chapter 29 — Gears and Ghosts

Rambleon began the night in Tinker Town, same stone and steam he'd ended in, and before he'd gone far, Cassidy had fallen in beside him, familiar as ever after so many roads walked together. Others drifted into the column too — Wes, Maru, Banished, Natalya — a loose knot of strangers met at the city's edge, there and then gone almost as quickly. Together they rode the tram out, climbed back through the Gates of Ironforge, and walked the frozen shore of Iceflow Lake before turning down into Gnomeregan itself.

The gnome city swallowed them for a while. Rambleon let Ink Supplies go, not worth the chasing, and pushed on through the Clockwerk Run into the Hall of Gears, where he and the others brought Grubbis down. It didn't last. Wes, Natalya, and Banished peeled away one after another, and Rambleon climbed back up into daylight — Stormwind's Trade District, bright after so much machinery and dark.

Cassidy found him there again, and what followed felt like déjà vu made solid: the very circuit they'd walked the night before, retraced step for step — Elwynn's hush, the Burning Steppes' heat, Searing Gorge's ash, Dun Morogh's cold, the Great Forge's glow — as though his boots had simply remembered the way without asking him. He paused for pictures at the same old waypoints, markers laid over markers now.

Back at Gnomeregan's gates two more hunters, Ia and Stryker, joined him, along with a druid named Annie who was gone again inside a minute. The deeper tunnels were kinder to his pockets than before: a two-hander off a bandit, a staff off something called a gorilla, the Sparklematic 5200 and its sequel task both finished and struck from the ledger, and later still a weapon worth calling rare, pulled from the wreck of a thing named Viscous Fallout. He let Highland Hides and two other old obligations go while he was at it, lightening the load. Twice that night the tunnels killed him, and twice he woke under Kharanos's sky and walked back in.

Stryker parted from him first, then Ia, and by the time Rambleon reached the Trade District again it was only Cassidy left at his shoulder — until she, too, took her leave. He paused long enough for one last picture before calling it a night, no higher in level than when he'd started, but richer by a little steel and a lot of walking.
