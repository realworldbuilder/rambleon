This is the record of Rambleon Birdsong, a Night Elf hunter, kept across twenty-three nights between late September and mid-October, carrying him from the uncertain ground of level eight to the threshold of level twenty-eight. What follows is not the fastest road through Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms, nor the cleverest — only the one he actually walked, night by night, exactly as he set it down.

At Lake Al'Ameth he turned in Denalan's Earth, then two small errands among the timberlings, Timberling Sprouts and Timberling Seeds, while the glade's lashers and elder timberlings gave him real trouble, alongside nightsabers and a webwood lurker near The Cleft.
