This is the record of Rambleon Birdsong, a night elf hunter, from level eight to level twenty, drawn from eleven nights of play logged between September 21 and October 2, 2026. It traces, stretch by stretch, the road he actually walked — not the fastest one, not the wisest, just his.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

Rambleon began the record at level eight, moving between Dolanaar, Starbreeze Village, Lake Al'Ameth, and the Cleft. He picked up Seek Redemption!, Gnarlpine Corruption, the timberling errands of Sprouts and Seeds, Rellian Greenspyre, and The Relics of Wakening, and over the course of the night turned in Zenn's Bidding, A Troubling Breeze, Denalan's Earth, the timberling quests, Seek Redemption!, Gnarlpine Corruption, Twisted Hatred, and a bounty on the Gnarlpine Furbolg. Along the way he met nightsabers, webwood lurkers, gnarlpine gardeners, warriors and ursa, timberlings and their lasher sproutlings, a befouled webwood, and Xethorr the Wicked. Hazardelf and Tiamaat shared some of the ground with him, though he noted working the lake largely by himself — "alone at the lake slayin timberlings," as he put it. He closed the night at level nine, there at Lake Al'Ameth.

From Teldrassil Rambleon passed into Darnassus, his first real look at the city, moving through Warrior's Terrace, the Cenarion Enclave, the Temple of the Moon, and Craftsmen's Terrace.
