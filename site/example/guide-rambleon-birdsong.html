This is the record of Rambleon Birdsong, a night elf hunter, as he walked from level eight to level twenty-one across twelve nights in Azeroth. What follows is not advice, and it is not the best road — only the one he actually took, set down in the order it happened.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

The record opens at Dolanaar, where Rambleon settled Zenn's Bidding and set out for Starbreeze Village and A Troubling Breeze. At Lake Al'Ameth he answered Denalan's Earth and the paired errands of the Timberlings, sprouts and seeds, while nightsabers and a webwood lurker crossed his path. The Gnarlpines kept him busiest of all — gardener, warrior, and ursa — and at the Cleft he faced something called Xethorr the Wicked, alongside a bounty on their furbolg kin. Hazardelf and Tiamaat walked with him through much of it, though he noted one spell alone at the lake, slaying timberlings by himself. He picked up word of Rellian Greenspyre and the Relics of Wakening, errands for another day, and by the time Seek Redemption!, Gnarlpine Corruption, and Twisted Hatred were settled, he had climbed to level nine at the lake.

The coast was not gentle — ten deaths across the Vale, the Excavation, Mist's Edge, Bashal'Aran, and the Tower — but Strafhe, Banne, and Cassidy shared the road, and pictures mark both the landing and each level gained.
