Rambleon Birdsong came to Teldrassil's shores at level 8, a Night Elf hunter with a bow and more road ahead of him than he knew. What follows is the account of the thirteen nights that carried him to level 22 — through Teldrassil and Darkshore, into Darnassus and Ashenvale, down to Stormwind and Westfall and the Deadmines, and back to the moonlit edges of Ashenvale again. It is only what happened, in the order it happened.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

The road began in Dolanaar, where Rambleon settled Zenn's Bidding and carried A Troubling Breeze out toward Starbreeze Village. At Lake Al'Ameth he closed out Denalan's Earth and the paired tasks of the Timberling Sprouts and Seeds, taking up word of Rellian Greenspyre and The Relics of Wakening as he went. Nightsabers and webwood lurkers gave way to gnarlpine gardeners, warriors, and ursa; timberlings and lasher sproutlings crowded the lakeshore, and a befouled webwood and the furbolg Xethorr the Wicked passed beneath his arrows. Hazardelf and Tiamaat shared stretches of the evening with him. Back in Dolanaar he finished Seek Redemption!, Gnarlpine Corruption, Twisted Hatred, and a furbolg bounty, and climbed to level 9 at the lake — though for a time, as he put it himself, he had simply been alone at the lake, slaying timberlings.
