This is the record of Rambleon Birdsong, a Night Elf hunter, kept across ten nights as he climbed from level 8 to level 20 — the road actually walked, not the best one, through Teldrassil, Darnassus, Darkshore, Ashenvale, the Barrens, Stonetalon, Stormwind, Westfall, and Ironforge, in the order it happened.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

The journal opens in Teldrassil, on a night that ran only an hour and four minutes. Rambleon worked the ground between Dolanaar, Starbreeze Village, and Lake Al'Ameth, turning in Zenn's Bidding, A Troubling Breeze, and Denalan's Earth, then clearing Timberling Sprouts and Timberling Seeds where he'd picked them up. He fought his way through Nightsabers, Webwood Lurkers, and a run of Gnarlpine furbolgs — Gardener, Warrior, Ursa — along with Timberlings, a Lasher Sproutling, a Befouled Webwood, and Xethorr the Wicked. Hazardelf and Tiamaat kept him company for part of it, but by Lake Al'Ameth he was on his own, and said so plainly: "alone at the lake slayin timberlings." He closed the night in Dolanaar, turning in Seek Redemption! and Gnarlpine Corruption, and climbed to level 9.

The next stretch carried Rambleon back through Darnassus into Teldrassil's wilder corners, nearly three hours with a companion named Bird. At the Oracle Glade he closed out The Lost Runner, then The Enchanted Glade, crossing to level 10 near Wellspring River after taking up Not Dead Yet and a clutch of beast-taming errands. Dolanaar absorbed most of what followed — Not Dead Yet, three rounds of Taming the Beast, Camping 101: Cooking, and Recipe of the Kaldorei all turned in there, and it was in Dolanaar that he reached level 11, a moment worth a picture. Only eighteen minutes this time, but Darnassus earned a line in the journal. Rambleon closed out Tumors in the Cenarion Enclave, handed in Training the Beast, and brought Tears of the Moon to the Temple of the Moon, picking up Nessa Shadowsong, Return to Denalan, and Sathrah's Sacrifice to carry back out.
