This is the road Rambleon Birdsong walked, a Night Elf Hunter climbing from level 8 to level 23 across fifteen recorded nights in Azeroth. It is set down here exactly as his journal kept it — not the quickest path, not the wisest, only his.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

He began the night already level 8, moving between Dolanaar, Starbreeze Village, Lake Al'Ameth, and the Cleft. The work was the Gnarlpines' corruption and the Timberlings' strange sproutings — furbolgs and lashers and a creature called Xethorr the Wicked crossing his path alongside nightsabers and webwood lurkers. Hazardelf and Tiamaat shared the paths with him for stretches, though he wrote of one patch of it plainly: alone at the lake, slaying timberlings. By the time Denalan's earth and the sprouts and seeds were handed in, the Gnarlpine bounty claimed, he had climbed to level 9 at Lake Al'Ameth — a small, solitary accomplishment at the water's edge.
