This is the record of six nights with Rambleon Birdsong, a night elf hunter of Teldrassil, from the evening he stood at level eight to the night, six chapters and sixteen hours and twenty-seven minutes later, that he crossed into level fifteen at Ameth'Aran. It follows the road exactly as he walked it — Teldrassil to Darnassus and back, and at the end, the crossing to Darkshore — no shortcuts, no better path, only his.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

He began that first night at level eight, walking into Dolanaar with quests to settle. At Starbreeze Village he turned in A Troubling Breeze, and out along Lake Al'Ameth he closed out Denalan's Earth, Timberling Sprouts, and Timberling Seeds, the timberlings themselves rising to meet him along with lashers and a stray webwood lurker. He picked up Rellian Greenspyre and The Relics of Wakening before the night was through, and fought past gnarlpine gardeners, warriors, and an ursa, and once a nightsaber crossed his path. Hazardelf and Tiamaat rode with him for stretches, but for the worst of it, at the lake, he was on his own — "alone at the lake slayin timberlings," he wrote. It was there, among the fronds, that he crossed into level nine. He closed the night finishing Gnarlpine Corruption, Twisted Hatred, and a bounty on the Gnarlpine furbolg back at Dolanaar.

2DarnassusLevel 9

Warrior's Terrace · Cenarion Enclave · Temple of the Moon · Craftsmen's Terrace · passing through Teldrassil
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake, Chapter 2 — The Oracle Glade · September 21, 2026 – September 22, 2026 · 2h 40m

The path from Teldrassil led him into Darnassus for the first time in the record, and he crossed the Warrior's Terrace and the Craftsmen's Terrace before finding his way to the Cenarion Enclave, where he turned in Rellian Greenspyre. He passed by the Temple of the Moon as well, and picked up a new task called Tumors before the night closed. Tiamaat was still beside him for this quiet errand into the city, a small stop between longer stretches in the wood.

3TeldrassilLevel 9–11

The Oracle Glade · Wellspring River · Wellspring Lake · Dolanaar · Lake Al'Ameth · passing through Darnassus
From Chapter 2 — The Oracle Glade, Chapter 3 — The Calm of Darnassus · September 22, 2026 – September 23, 2026 · 2h 53m

Back in Teldrassil, Rambleon returned to the Oracle Glade to close out The Lost Runner, and picked up The Enchanted Glade there as well, a task he finished at level ten. Along Wellspring River he tamed his beasts — three times over, the record shows — and by the time he reached that river's edge he had climbed to level ten himself, though the bloodfeather harpies and a Lady Sathrah among the Wellspring reeds cost him his life more than once; he died three times between the Oracle Glade and the river before it was done. Dolanaar absorbed the aftermath: Not Dead Yet, Camping 101: Cooking, and Recipe of the Kaldorei all turned in there, and it was in Dolanaar, with Bird at his side, that he reached level eleven, a moment he thought worth a picture. At Lake Al'Ameth he closed The Shimmering Frond and The Sprouted Fronds before the night was out.

4DarnassusLevel 11

Warrior's Terrace · Cenarion Enclave · Craftsmen's Terrace · Temple of the Moon · Tradesmen's Terrace
From Chapter 3 — The Calm of Darnassus, Chapter 4 — The Barrow Den · September 23, 2026 – September 24, 2026 · 18m

He came back to Darnassus at level eleven, and this time lingered — a picture marks his entry into the city, and another a quiet moment in the Cenarion Enclave, where he finally closed out Tumors. He also turned in Training the Beast and, at the Temple of the Moon, Tears of the Moon. Walking the Warrior's Terrace, the Craftsmen's Terrace, and the Tradesmen's Terrace, he picked up new business for later — Nessa Shadowsong, Return to Denalan, Sathrah's Sacrifice — but for now the city itself was the point. "i really like darnassus," he wrote, "very calm. never overcrowded." It was the shortest stop of the record, eighteen minutes, and he seemed glad enough to spend them there before moving on.

5TeldrassilLevel 11–13

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Shadowglen · Aldrassil · Shadowthread Cave · Lake Al'Ameth · Ban'ethil Hollow · Ban'ethil Barrow Den · Pools of Arlithrien · Gnarlpine Hold · Wellspring Lake · The Oracle Glade · passing through Darnassus
From Chapter 4 — The Barrow Den, Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore · September 24, 2026 – September 25, 2026 · 5h 39m

The longest stretch of the whole record ran back through Teldrassil, from Dolanaar down through Shadowglen and Aldrassil, where he closed Fang of Githyiss and Tenaron's Summons, out to the Ban'ethil Barrow Den, where the Sleeping Druid and Druid of the Claw quests waited and where, fighting past gnarlpine shamans and a creature called Rageclaw, he died twice before reaching level twelve. Crown of the Earth, turned in four times over at Dolanaar, and Oakenscowl and The Great Tree Provides at Lake Al'Ameth filled out the middle hours. He was rarely alone here — Cassidy, Aurel, Vamshi, Titania, Kaeds, Dread, Abbey, Lepoof, and Highlord all crossed his path in company at one point or another. Ferocitas the Dream Eater fell to him at Dolanaar, and at the Oracle Glade he closed Darkness in the Glade and The Oracle Tree, reaching level thirteen there. "turning in a couple quests then turning in for the night," he wrote.

6DarnassusLevel 13

Warrior's Terrace · Craftsmen's Terrace · Cenarion Enclave
From Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore · September 25, 2026 · 10m

A brief return to Darnassus followed, ten minutes only, the kind of stop that exists to clear the ledger before moving on. It was the shortest visit to the city yet. He crossed the Warrior's Terrace and Craftsmen's Terrace to reach the Cenarion Enclave, where he turned in the quest simply called Teldrassil, and picked up Grove of the Ancients to carry with him. A picture marks his entry into the city once more — by now a familiar doorway rather than a discovery.

7TeldrassilLevel 13

Rut'theran Village · The Veiled Sea
From Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore · September 25, 2026 · 1m

Barely a minute passed at Rut'theran Village, but it mattered: he turned in Nessa Shadowsong and The Bounty of Teldrassil there, at the edge of the Veiled Sea, and picked up Flight to Auberdine in the same breath. It was less a stretch than a threshold — the last errand of the island before the water carried him somewhere else entirely, the record barely pausing long enough to note it. If you take the same road, that dock is where Teldrassil ends and the sea begins.

8DarkshoreLevel 13–15

The Veiled Sea · Mist's Edge · Auberdine · The Long Wash · Twilight Shore · Twilight Vale · Wildbend River · Grove of the Ancients · The Master's Glaive · Remtravel's Excavation · Bashal'Aran · Ameth'Aran · Cliffspring River · Tower of Althalaxx · Ruins of Mathystra
From Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore, Chapter 6 — Across the Border to Ashenvale · September 25, 2026 – September 26, 2026 · 8h 02m

The sea carried him to Darkshore, and Auberdine became his anchor for the last long stretch of the record — seven hours and more, the longest of any night. He turned in Washed Ashore twice over, Buzzbox 827, Beached Sea Turtle, How Big a Threat?, and Thundris Windweaver there, with Buzzbox 411 closed at Mist's Edge and the Bashal'Aran tasks settled at Bashal'Aran itself, where greymist raiders and seers, moonstalkers, and thistle bears met him along the shore. Grove of the Ancients he closed near where he'd picked it up, and level fourteen came somewhere in Darkshore's turning. Strafhe, Banne, and Cassidy shared parts of the road with him, which mattered, for the road was hard: fifteen deaths across Twilight Vale, Remtravel's Excavation, Mist's Edge, and Bashal'Aran, more than the rest of the record combined. Still he pressed on, closing The Fall of Ameth'Aran and Tools of the Highborne, reached level fifteen at Ameth'Aran — six nights out from Dolanaar.
