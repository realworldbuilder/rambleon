This is the record of Rambleon Birdsong's road from level eight to level thirty, kept across twenty-seven nights in Azeroth. It begins in the quiet of Teldrassil and ends, many roads later, in the depths of a Stormwind gaol — not the fastest path, not the wisest, but his own, written down as he walked it so that another traveler might recognize the ground beneath their own feet.

"Alone at the lake slayin timberlings," he noted, and there was a certain satisfaction in that solitude.
