This is the record of Rambleon Birdsong, a night elf hunter, from level eight to level seventeen across seven nights. It is not a guide to the best road, only the one he actually walked.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

Rambleon moved between Dolanaar, Starbreeze Village, and the mossy round of Lake Al'Ameth, already at level eight and settling into the rhythm of an evening's work. He closed out small burdens as he found them — Zenn's Bidding, A Troubling Breeze, Denalan's Earth — and picked up others in their place, including word of gnarlpine corruption and Rellian Greenspyre's plea. Nightsabers and webwood lurkers troubled him little, though the gnarlpines pressed harder, gardener and warrior and ursa alike. Somewhere among the timberlings he crossed the lake's edge alone. "alone at the lake slayin timberlings," he wrote, and there's a quiet pride in that line. Hazardelf and Tiamaat crossed his path before Xethorr the Wicked fell and the corruption quest closed. Level nine came to him at the lake, the reward for a steady night's work.

2DarnassusLevel 9

From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake, Chapter 2 — The Oracle Glade · September 21, 2026 – September 22, 2026 · 2h 40m

The road from Teldrassil brought him down into Darnassus, unhurried — Warrior's Terrace, the Craftsmen's Terrace, the hush of the Temple of the Moon. In the Cenarion Enclave he closed Rellian Greenspyre's errand at last, and picked up word of tumors needing attention before moving on. Tiamaat was still with him for this crossing, the two of them threading the city's terraces together before Teldrassil called him back.

3TeldrassilLevel 9–11

The Oracle Glade · Wellspring River · Wellspring Lake · Dolanaar · Lake Al'Ameth · passing through Darnassus
From Chapter 2 — The Oracle Glade, Chapter 3 — The Calm of Darnassus · September 22, 2026 – September 23, 2026 · 2h 53m

Back through Darnassus and out again, Rambleon found new ground at the Oracle Glade, closing the Lost Runner's task and taking up the Enchanted Glade's troubles before turning toward Wellspring River. Bloodfeather harpies, rogues, and sorceresses tested him there, and so did the river's own Elder Nightsabers; he died three times between the river and the glade before level ten found him. Timberlings had grown fiercer too, tramplers and mire beasts among them, by the time he reached Dolanaar to tame beasts of his own and settle accounts — Not Dead Yet, a cooking recipe, the sprouted fronds back at Lake Al'Ameth. Bird walked beside him for part of this stretch. Level eleven came in Dolanaar, and a picture was taken to mark it, a small deserved vanity after Lady Sathrah and the feral nightsabers of the glade.

A brief return to the city, only eighteen minutes of it, but enough to close the tumor errand in the Cenarion Enclave, settle Training the Beast, and lay the Tears of the Moon quest to rest at the Temple of the Moon. 5TeldrassilLevel 11–13

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Shadowglen · Aldrassil · Shadowthread Cave · Lake Al'Ameth · Ban'ethil Hollow · Ban'ethil Barrow Den · Pools of Arlithrien · Gnarlpine Hold · Wellspring Lake · The Oracle Glade · passing through Darnassus
From Chapter 4 — The Barrow Den, Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore · September 24, 2026 – September 25, 2026 · 5h 39m

The longest single night yet carried him from Dolanaar through Shadowglen and Aldrassil, where he closed the fang of Githyiss and Tenaron's summons, then down into Ban'ethil Barrow Den chasing the Sleeping Druid and the Druid of the Claw. Oakenscowl and a scatter of gnarlpines — shaman, defender, augur, mystic — met him along the way, and Ferocitas the Dream Eater by the time he reached the Oracle Glade to close Darkness in the Glade and the Oracle Tree itself. He died twice in the Barrow Den before level twelve took hold there; level thirteen followed at the Glade. Cassidy, Aurel, Vamshi, Titania, Kaeds, Dread, Abbey, Lepoof, and Highlord all shared some part of the road with him, a crowded stretch after so much solitary walking. "turning in a couple quests then turning in for the night," he wrote, closing a full night.

6DarnassusLevel 13

Warrior's Terrace · Craftsmen's Terrace · Cenarion Enclave
From Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore · September 25, 2026 · 10m

Ten minutes only, a quick pass through Warrior's Terrace and the Craftsmen's Terrace on the way to the Cenarion Enclave, where he turned in the quest simply called Teldrassil, the island handing its own name back to the city that watched over it. It was a brief visit, businesslike, nothing like the leisurely wandering of nights before, but Darnassus never asked for more than what a traveler had time to give.

8DarkshoreLevel 13–15

The Veiled Sea · Mist's Edge · Auberdine · The Long Wash · Twilight Shore · Twilight Vale · Wildbend River · Grove of the Ancients · The Master's Glaive · Remtravel's Excavation · Bashal'Aran · Ameth'Aran · Cliffspring River · Tower of Althalaxx · Ruins of Mathystra
From Chapter 5 — The Shores of Darkshore, Chapter 6 — Across the Border to Ashenvale · September 25, 2026 – September 26, 2026 · 8h 02m

The crossing brought him to Auberdine and the long grey coast of Darkshore, and the work there was steady and various: washed-ashore sailors, buzzboxes, a red crystal, a fishing pole owed to someone waiting. Greymist raiders and coastrunners worked the shoreline, and further in, past Bashal'Aran and the Tower of Althalaxx, he tangled with blackwood windtalkers and pathfinders, moonstalkers, thistle bears grizzled and rabid. It was costly ground — seventeen deaths scattered across Twilight Vale, Remtravel's Excavation, Mist's Edge, Bashal'Aran, and the Tower itself — but he kept returning to Auberdine to trade what he'd found for what he needed. Level fourteen arrived somewhere in Darkshore's sprawl, level fifteen at Ameth'Aran. Strafhe and Banne walked with him for part of this, Cassidy for another. Pictures caught his entry into Darkshore and both level-ups, proof he'd been there and had climbed.

10DarkshoreLevel 15–17

The Veiled Sea · Mist's Edge · Auberdine · The Long Wash · Twilight Shore · Twilight Vale · The Master's Glaive · Grove of the Ancients · Wildbend River · Remtravel's Excavation · Bashal'Aran · Cliffspring River · Ameth'Aran · Blackwood Den
From Chapter 6 — Across the Border to Ashenvale · September 26, 2026 · 7h 55m

He returned to the same coast for what became the longest night of the record, back to Auberdine's docks with more beached creatures and turtles to carry in, and word of plagued lands and an infection needing cleansing. Murkdeep himself turned up among the greymist warriors, netters, and tidehunters along the shore, and further inland, at Blackwood Den, blackwood warriors and rabid thistle bears met him as he chased down a lost master. Eleven deaths marked the toll, at Twilight Shore and at the Den. Caerwyn, Reighna, Elentria, Softtaco, Menos, Cassidy, and Noseeum all shared parts of the road with him, the fullest company of the whole record. Level sixteen came at Bashal'Aran, level seventeen at Twilight Vale, both caught in pictures, the second closing out the night's long climb.

11AshenvaleLevel 17

Maestra's Post · Astranaar
From Chapter 6 — Across the Border to Ashenvale, Chapter 7 — The Long Wash to Stonetalon · September 26, 2026 – September 27, 2026 · 9m

The record closes with a short crossing into Ashenvale, past a Den Mother and the last moonstalkers and blackwood warriors of Darkshore, to Maestra's Post, where he closed the Tower of Althalaxx at last. At Astranaar he turned in the trek that had brought him there and picked up word of the Zoram Strand and trouble in Stonetalon, threads left for whatever came next. Cassidy, steady company across so many of these nights, was with him again, and so was Tina. A picture marked his entry into Ashenvale, and another his return to Darkshore as the seventh night closed — level seventeen, and the record's end, not a triumph so much as a place to stop and look back at the road already walked.
