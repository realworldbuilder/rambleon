Rambleon Birdsong came to Teldrassil as every night elf does, young and curious under the great tree, and left it twenty-nine nights later a seasoned hand of thirty levels, having walked from the quiet paths around Dolanaar to the twisting dark beneath Ironforge and the clattering tunnels of Gnomeregan. This is the road he actually walked, in the order the nights gave it to him — not the fastest way, not the wisest, only his.
