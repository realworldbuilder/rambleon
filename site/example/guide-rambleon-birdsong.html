This is the road Rambleon Birdsong walked, a night elf hunter of Teldrassil, from level eight to level nineteen, across eight nights recorded between the twenty-first and twenty-eighth of September. What follows is not the wisest path nor the fastest, only the one his boots actually took, stretch by stretch, in the order the world gave it to him.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

He began in Dolanaar, still finding his feet at level eight, quests piling into his pack faster than he could answer them — word of gnarlpines, of a breeze gone wrong at Starbreeze Village, of timberlings sprouting where they should not. He worked outward to Lake Al'Ameth with Hazardelf and Tiamaat at his side, though for a stretch he found himself without them, alone among the sprouted things. "alone at the lake slayin timberlings," he wrote after, the words carrying more weight than complaint — a young hunter measuring himself against saplings and spiders and a thing calling itself Xethorr the Wicked. By the time he turned for Dolanaar again, gnarlpine furbolg were falling to his bow, and he crossed into level nine with the Cleft's quiet behind him.
