Rambleon Birdsong is a Night Elf hunter, and this is the road he walked, from his eighth level to his twentieth, across nine nights in Azeroth. It is not the fastest path nor the wisest one, only the one his boots actually wore into the grass, quest by quest, death by death, from Teldrassil's forests to Darkshore's coast and on to the human lands beyond.

He closed out Zenn's Bidding there, then carried A Troubling Breeze to Starbreeze Village and Denalan's Earth down to Lake Al'Ameth, where the timberlings grew thick around the water. Twisted Hatred and a bounty on gnarlpine furbolg followed back in Dolanaar, and somewhere between the timberlings and a creature called Xethorr the Wicked, level nine arrived at the lake — a first small notch in a night that had started at eight.
