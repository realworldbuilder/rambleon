Rambleon Birdsong came to Teldrassil a Night Elf hunter of the eighth level, green enough still to be startled by a timberling, and he left these pages a creature of the twenty-eighth, having walked from the Veiled Sea to the Great Sea and most of the green country between. Twenty-two nights hold the whole of it — eighty-eight hours and some minutes, by his own accounting — and what follows is the road as he actually trod it, turn by turn, with nothing added and nothing explained away.

He began where the Night Elves begin, among the quiet avenues of Dolanaar, with Zenn's Bidding and A Troubling Breeze already laid at his feet. The work at first was small and strange — Gnarlpine Corruption, Timberling Sprouts and Seeds, the business of Denalan's Earth — and it took him from the village out to Starbreeze and down to the glass stillness of Lake Al'Ameth. There he fought nightsabers and webwood lurkers, Gnarlpine gardeners and warriors and ursa, the lashers and timberlings that guarded the water's edge, and one named foe, Xethorr the Wicked, fell to him among them. Hazardelf and Tiamaat shared the work with him some of that time, though he wrote of one stretch spent apart: alone at the lake, slaying timberlings, ninth level reached before the night's hour was out.
