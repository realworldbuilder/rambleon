Rambleon Birdsong was eight levels into his life on Teldrassil when this record begins, a young night elf hunter with the island's quiet trails still mostly unwalked. Sixteen nights later he stood in the Wetlands at level twenty-four, a long road behind him through forest, coast, mountain, and mine. What follows is that road, as he walked it.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

He began among the familiar green of Dolanaar, carrying small errands outward into Starbreeze Village and down to the stillness of Lake Al'Ameth. There were timberlings to put down and furbolgs skulking at the wood's edge, gnarlpine gardeners and warriors and the stranger shapes of Xethorr the Wicked and the webwood lurkers in their silk. Hazardelf and Tiamaat kept him company for stretches of it, but one note from that first night stayed plain and unembellished: alone at the lake, slaying timberlings. By the lake's edge he passed into level nine, a small, solitary milestone earned in Teldrassil's hush.
