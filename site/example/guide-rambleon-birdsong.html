This is the road Rambleon Birdsong walked from level eight to level twenty, Night Elf hunter, over ten nights in Azeroth — a plain accounting of what he fought, who walked beside him, and what he turned in, set down in the order it happened.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

The record opens in Dolanaar, where old business came due: Zenn's Bidding closed out, A Troubling Breeze answered at Starbreeze Village, and at Lake Al'Ameth both Denalan's Earth and a pair of timberling errands handed in almost at once. The woods around him were thick with trouble — nightsabers and a webwood lurker, Gnarlpine gardeners, warriors, and ursa among the trees, a befouled webwood, and something called Xethorr the Wicked. Hazardelf and Tiamaat shared the road with him for part of it, though at the lake he worked alone for a stretch — "alone at the lake slayin timberlings," he wrote, plain and unbothered. He picked up word of Rellian Greenspyre and the relics of wakening, turned in Seek Redemption!, the Gnarlpine Corruption, and Twisted Hatred, and by the lake's edge had climbed to level nine.

From Teldrassil he crossed into Darnassus for the first time in the record, Tiamaat still at his side, and walked its terraces — Warrior's Terrace, Craftsmen's Terrace, the Temple of the Moon. From Chapter 2 — The Oracle Glade, Chapter 3 — The Calm of Darnassus · September 22, 2026 – September 23, 2026 · 2h 53m

Back in Teldrassil, with Bird for company now, Rambleon pushed into ground he hadn't walked before — the Oracle Glade, the Wellspring River, its lake. He closed out the Enchanted Glade at the Glade itself, then returned to Dolanaar to settle Not Dead Yet, three rounds of Taming the Beast, a cooking lesson, and a Kaldorei recipe, finishing the Shimmering Frond back at the lake. The country gave him new names to learn by fighting them: strigid hunters, an elder nightsaber, timberling tramplers and mire beasts, bloodfeather rogues and their kin, a webwood silkspinner, an elder timberling, a feral nightsaber, and Lady Sathrah among the rest. He died three times, at the river and again at the Glade, and climbed two levels regardless — ten at the Wellspring River, eleven at Dolanaar, a moment that earned a picture.

From Chapter 3 — The Calm of Darnassus, Chapter 4 — The Barrow Den · September 23, 2026 – September 24, 2026 · 18m

A short visit this time, under twenty minutes across two chapters, but it carried weight: the tumors he'd been given were turned in at last at the Cenarion Enclave, Training the Beast settled, and Tears of the Moon handed over at the Temple.
