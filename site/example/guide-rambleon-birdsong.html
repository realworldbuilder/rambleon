Rambleon Birdsong came to Azeroth as a night elf hunter, and this is the account of his first long road through it — from level eight to level twenty-nine, across twenty-four nights of play. It follows no one's advice but his own, quest by quest and death by death, in the order the road actually unspooled before him.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

The record opens in Dolanaar and Starbreeze Village, where Rambleon began the ordinary business of a young hunter at level eight: clearing gnarlpine corruption, answering Zenn's bidding, carrying word of a troubling breeze back to the village that had raised it. At Lake Al'Ameth he turned over Denalan's earth and the first timberling sprouts and seeds, and there, among nightsabers and webwood lurkers, gnarlpine gardeners and warriors and ursa, a befouled webwood and a creature called Xethorr the Wicked, he found his way to level nine. Hazardelf and Tiamaat were with him for some of it, though not all. One note from that first night stayed plain: alone at the lake, slaying timberlings. Rellian Greenspyre and a bounty on gnarlpine furbolgs closed the stretch, and twisted hatred besides.
