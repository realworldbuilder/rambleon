Rambleon Birdsong was eight levels into his life on Azeroth when this record opens, a young hunter still finding the shape of his own competence among the roots of Teldrassil. Over twenty-eight nights, from the twenty-first of September to the nineteenth of October, he walked and sailed and flew his way to thirty, keeping notes as he went — sparse ones, mostly, but honest. What follows is not the quickest road or the wisest one. It is only his: the lakes he fished monsters out of, the companions who rode beside him for an hour or a night, the cities he passed through without lingering, and the deaths that taught him nothing he wrote down except that they had happened. Walk it as he walked it, in order, and you will have walked his Azeroth.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

He began at Dolanaar, still new enough to count his levels like coins. The notes from that first night are thin but telling — "alone at the lake slayin timberlings," he wrote of Lake Al'Ameth, and there is a particular satisfaction in that line, the quiet pride of a hunter who did not need company to be useful. Hazardelf and Tiamaat crossed his path there and at Starbreeze Village, where the troubling breeze that had worried the village was finally laid to rest. Gnarlpine warriors and ursa, webwood lurkers, nightsabers and the bandit Xethorr the Wicked all fell to his bow in turn. By the lake's edge he passed into level nine, the Cleft behind him and Dolanaar's errands cleared from his ledger.
