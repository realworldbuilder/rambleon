Rambleon Birdsong began this stretch of his road a young night elf of eight winters' training, bow in hand and still finding his feet in Teldrassil's dark. Twenty nights later he stood in Arathi Highlands and the Wetlands a hunter of twenty-seven, with a shadowmeld's worth of names, lakes, and long walks behind him.

1TeldrassilLevel 8–9

Dolanaar · Starbreeze Village · Lake Al'Ameth · The Cleft
From Chapter 1 — Alone at the Lake · September 21, 2026 · 1h 04m

He started among the familiar close trees of Dolanaar, the kind of ground a young hunter learns before he learns anything else. Hazardelf and Tiamaat walked beside him for a time, but much of the work was solitary — gnarlpines put down, timberlings cut back at Lake Al'Ameth, a nightsaber and a webwood lurker among the first things he'd ever had to fight. He wrote of himself there plainly: alone at the lake, slaying timberlings. It was unglamorous work, and he did it anyway, turning in favor after favor at Zenn's and Starbreeze until Gnarlpine Corruption and Twisted Hatred closed out, and the Lake gave him his ninth level.
