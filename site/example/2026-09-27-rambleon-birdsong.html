Chapter 7 — Ilkrud Magthrull's Tome

Rambleon Birdsong · September 27, 2026 · 4h 15m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney
← Chapter 6 — Across the Border to Ashenvale

Rambleon had meant, the night before, to let Ashenvale's newness carry him toward Zoram Strand and Stonetalon, but Astranaar found him doubling back instead, Cassidy at his side again within the minute — familiar as breath, three nights and more of walking together behind them now. The road bent them across the border into Darkshore once more, through Twilight Shore and the Long Wash to Auberdine, and on toward Bashal'Aran and the Cliffspring, where a moonstalker and a blackwood warrior fell before a priest named Tina joined them just long enough to see a den mother's lair emptied. Then both women went their own ways, and Auberdine kept him waiting the better part of an hour before Cassidy came back to him.

Together they pushed deeper into Twilight Vale, past Ameth'Aran's old stones and along the Wildbend, into the tangled ground of the Master's Glaive. Twilight Disciples and their thugs gave way there, word of a *Therylune's Escape* taken up but not yet answered, and for a short while a priest called Papa walked with them near Remtravel's dig before the moonstalkers came thick — a matriarch, her runts, a sire — and the Glaive took its due: he fell twice in that tangle before rising and pressing on.

Ashenvale opened around him again past Maestra's Post, and cost him one more death for the trouble before he found his feet in Astranaar and turned back to finish what waited. At the Ruins of Ordil'Aran, with Papa beside him a second time, he broke the tower's soul gem free at last and closed out the long business of the Tower of Althalaxx — only to find the tale wasn't done with him yet.

The Shrine of Aessina gave him his eighteenth level, quiet as ever, worth a picture and a private nod. Wildthorn stalkers and ghostpaw runners thinned before him, though the shrine claimed him three times before it let him keep the ground, and a druid named Karis, new to him, arrived in time to see the last of those falls. Fire Scar Shrine finished the night's reckoning — felslayers, lesser felguards, and at the heart of it Ilkrud Magthrull himself, who yielded his tome for the tower's tally before the flames there claimed Rambleon four times over. Ten deaths in all by the time he stood again in Auberdine, tired, eighteenth level settled on him like something earned rather than given.
