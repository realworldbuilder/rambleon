Chapter 23 — The Long Loop Back to Southshore

Rambleon Birdsong · October 14, 2026 · 3h 42m in Azeroth

Rambleon left the Lion's Pride Inn the way he had before, the fire there still warm in memory, and found Cassidy waiting to fall in step with him again — the same easy rhythm the two of them had kept since Aldrassil, needing no word to begin. Together they waded the strands of Hillsbrad, the Torn Fin oracles and tidehunters falling in numbers that blurred into one long afternoon's work, and briefly a shaman named Eric walked with them, gone again within the hour. Farren's proof of murloc heads went to Marshall Redpath, and Southshore's business carried Rambleon out along the Eastern Strand, where the Daggerspine folk made him pay for the ground twice over — a walk back from the graveyard there, and another at Nethander Stead not long after, the kind of night where the dead don't stay down so much as get back up and keep walking.

A warrior named Fruit joined for a stretch and left a lockbox behind as thanks for the company. Stormwind Ho! sent Rambleon on the longest road of the night — through Dun Morogh's passes, down into Searing Gorge's heat, across the Burning Steppes to Elwynn's green quiet, and into Stormwind itself, where a warrior called Tyrone walked beside him long enough to see the missing diplomat's note delivered and Major Samuelson's report handed off at the Keep. A third death found him in Southshore itself before that errand was even finished, no more explained than the others, just another short walk home.

The road back to the Wetlands felt different once he was on it — Bluegill Marsh, then Sundown Marsh, and there among the Mosshide trappers and mystics the weight of experience finally tipped, level twenty-nine settling into him with the quiet satisfaction of a journeyman's garment finally fitting right. A picture was taken to mark it. Defender's gauntlets came out of the Mosshide Alpha's camp, good boar-worked leather, and Rambleon tried them on by firelight at Dun Modr before Thandol Span carried him into Arathi Highlands for the first time that night — new ground, briefly seen, before business pulled him back south to Refuge Pointe and down again to Southshore. A rogue and a paladin, Ninja and Steel, crossed his path for a scant two minutes near the end, barely long enough to trade nods, and then they were gone, and so was Cassidy, the night's longest companionship closing as quietly as it had opened.
