Chapter 15 — The Long Road to the Cathedral

Rambleon Birdsong · October 6, 2026 · 3h 47m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney
← Chapter 14 — New Shores, Familiar Company

Rambleon began the night alone at Deepwater Tavern, working his way out through Bluegill Marsh where crocolisks and murlocs gave way easily enough, the statuette errand for Karl Boran already taking its first steps. Cassidy found him before long, the ease between them picking up exactly where Menethil Harbor had left it the night before, and together they carried the quest on through Baradin Bay and the marsh beyond.

The real work waited at Angerfang Encampment. Natcho, a fellow hunter, fell in beside him there and stayed the better part of an hour while Dragonmaw raiders and bonewarders went down by the dozen and the camp's war banners came apart one by one. Dazzs, a rogue, joined for a quarter hour as the Horde's attack plans burned—Menethil Harbor, Southshore, Thelsamar, Ironforge, each struck from the list in turn—and Paladonald and Quad, paladin and mage, lent their strength for a few minutes more before the company scattered back to its separate roads. He fell eight times through the night, in the Dragonmaw camp at Angerfang and once among the whelps of the Green Belt, and each time walked back to finish what waited.

Statuette in hand, he carried it to Menethil Keep for Captain Stoutfist and raised a flagon at the Tavern before the road called him out again. Jaxsul, a shaman, walked with him through the Green Belt for near twenty minutes, and Kalos, a druid, stayed on through Direforge Hill as whelps red, crimson, and flamesnorting gave up good leather and good steel—boots, bracers, a cleaver worth keeping. The Pristine Crimson Scales came together at last, eight of eight, and Crocs of the Sky was done.

What followed was the longest road Rambleon had walked in some time: out of the Wetlands into Dun Morogh, through Ironforge's Great Forge, down past Kharanos into the heat of Searing Gorge and Burning Steppes, and on through Elwynn Forest to Stormwind itself. He paused for a picture at nearly every crossing, small marks of a long journey. In Stormwind Keep he laid the Menethil Statuette before Highlord Bolvar Fordragon, and carried it further still to the Cathedral of Light for Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker, where a shamed lieutenant's trouble waited to be taken up. Cassidy had gone her own way by then, as old friends sometimes do between one quest and the next, but the thread between them, he knew, was far from finished.
