Chapter 26 — The Long Ride to a New Door

Rambleon Birdsong · October 17, 2026 · 1h 54m in Azeroth
StoryRecapQuestsJourney
← Chapter 25 — Deeper Than Darnassus

Rambleon came back to Southshore that morning with the Baron's business still sitting unfinished where he'd left it the night before, a thread he meant to pick up again before long. But the day stretched quiet around him, hours passing with nothing to mark them, the kind of lull that follows a night as full as the one before had been. He did not mind it. Even a hunter needs a morning that asks nothing of him.

When he finally moved, he moved fast and far. He struck out from Southshore across the Eastern Strand and onto the Great Sea itself, the water carrying him clear from Hillsbrad into the Wetlands — Baradin Bay, then Menethil Harbor, then Menethil Bay, each a blur of dock and gull-cry he barely paused in. He paused only long enough at the water's edge to take a picture, the Wetlands opening wide and green behind him, before pressing on into Dun Morogh, dwarven stone rising cold and solid after so much salt air. He took another picture there, then threaded past the Gates of Ironforge, through Steelgrill's Depot and the little mountain town of Kharanos, never slowing to trade or rest.

From the Dun Morogh heights he turned south into country far rougher — Searing Gorge, its name earning itself in the picture he stopped to take, then on through Firewatch Ridge and past the mouth of Blackchar Cave without going in. Burning Steppes followed, ash-dark and close, a picture taken at the Altar of Storms and another glimpse of Draco'dar before he pushed through to softer ground. Elwynn Forest met him gently by comparison, green and familiar after so much stone and cinder, and he marked it too with a picture at Northshire Valley, the old starting country of so many Night Elves and others besides, though not his own.

It was Stormwind that closed the circuit. He came up through the Valley of Heroes and into the Trade District's noise and color, and there, almost without ceremony, he made the city his home — a small, deliberate act after a long day's wandering, choosing a hearth among humans rather than the moon-lit boughs of Teldrassil. He lingered a moment in the Canals after, the water quiet under the old stone bridges, before the day's long ride finally let him rest.
