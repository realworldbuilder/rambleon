Chapter 17 — Across the Sea to Hillsbrad

Rambleon Birdsong · October 8, 2026 · 2h 30m in Azeroth

Rambleon came back through Ironforge and down to Menethil as though his feet remembered the way without being told, and the Wetlands opened before him again — Whelgar's Excavation Site waiting just where he and Cassidy had left it the night before, though tonight he found it without her. Básdorcha, a hunter, fell in beside him there, and briefly Adryh, a paladin, and between them and the dig's Mottled Razormaws and Scytheclaws and Raptors, a Sarltooth's talon turned out to be the last piece Ormer needed. Somewhere in the counting of it Rambleon felt the quiet lift of a new year settling into his bones — twenty-five now, Raptor's End in hand, a picture taken to hold the moment, and a Novice Hunter's name to go with it.

He carried that lightness east to Thelgen Rock and the ferns of Mosshide Fen, where Falcon, a hunter, and Big, a paladin, and later Tzuyu, a druid, walked with him through mongrels and gnolls and fenrunners until the trappers' paws were accounted for. Back in the Green Belt he took up the Fire Taboo, and for a little while Fleche and Stoneheart, hunters both, ranged beside him against trappers and mistweavers and a Highland Raptor before the paths parted again, as they do with company met only for an evening.

The flint he needed waited in Ironbeard's Tomb, and the Wetlands walked him back from its graveyard more than once before the last of it was gathered, at Whelgar's earlier and near the tomb again now — though a pair of Thorbia's Gauntlets came out of the digging worth the trouble, and Stoneheart found him once more near the end, long enough to see the taboo lifted.

By Menethil Keep he had a new errand concerning the fall of Dun Modr, and the road doubled back through Dun Morogh to Ironforge, where he let go of half-finished business in Tinker Town — the fishing pole, the mushrooms, the prospector, all set down — for a rescue among lost gnomish data and a grander betrayal still to untangle, the Military Ward turning out gloves, a belt, a tunic, and leggings worth the wearing. The sea carried him somewhere new after that: Hillsbrad Foothills, its strands and Southshore pale enough to stop for a picture, a shamed lieutenant's business settled, a brewer's trade and a longer walk down the coast promised for another night, and Tom's gift carried home across the water to Bart Tidewater before the lamplight of Deepwater Tavern closed around him again, one more picture marking the moment — alone this time, but not unhappily so.
