Chapter 22 — The Curse Lifted

Rambleon Birdsong · October 13, 2026 · 4h 06m in Azeroth

Rambleon left Southshore again, but tonight the road doubled back on itself strangely — a blink through Dustwallow Marsh, the Barrens, Mulgore, Desolace, Stonetalon, before Ashenvale finally held him the way Hillsbrad had tried to the night before. Nightsong Woods received him like a familiar path, and there Cassidy fell in beside him — the druid he had ridden with a dozen nights before this one, since Aldrassil, and the two of them needed no introductions, only the easy quiet of people who already know each other's pace.

They cleared the Dor'Danil Barrow Den of its Forsaken dead, enough to carry off a bottle of their sickness as proof, and the Forest Song canopy was where the title of journeyman finally gave way — level twenty-eight settling into him quietly, a small satisfaction rather than a triumph. Satyrnaar's satyrs and Xavian's felsworn fell after, then the bears and totemics of Greenpaw's Foulweald, and a whale-marked cape and a suit of snapbrook armor came home in his pack for the trouble. Fallen Sky Lake gave up its moonstone before Cassidy and he turned west, through Darkshore and Teldrassil, up into Darnassus's quiet terraces, before the sea called them back toward Hillsbrad.

Southshore handed him two new errands before Menethil Harbor pulled him on to Auberdine's business — flowers gathered and delivered, on a word remembered from Sylessa. Raxen and Needsmo, two paladins and a druid he'd not keep, passed through as briefly as spray off Baradin Bay. It was The Lost Fleet that mattered most: the same cursed hulks he and Morrigan's company had first waded into two nights past, and this time, with Amba beside him a while, he finished it — Captain Halyndor fell, the curse lifted, and a strange eye came out of the wreck needing a priest's hand in Stormwind. Blinkín joined him there too, briefly, before the long road to the Cathedral of Light and back through Dun Morogh, Searing Gorge, and Elwynn Forest, cleansing the eye and chasing a missing diplomat along Stormwind's canals.

Hillsbrad and the Wetlands claimed him more than once before midnight — Splintertree Post, Hillsbrad Foothills, and The Lost Fleet twice over each took him down and sent him walking back. Pansar kept him company for the last of it, and when they parted at Baradin Bay, Rambleon turned for the Lion's Pride Inn, the fire there as good a place as any to let a long, far-wandering night come to rest.
