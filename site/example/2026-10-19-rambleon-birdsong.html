Chapter 28 — The Circuit

Rambleon left Stormwind that night the way a man sets out on a road he already knows by heart, and the road, as it turned out, was a long loop rather than a line. Cassidy joined him at the gates of the Trade District, the same steady company who had walked beside him through so many chapters now — Blackfathom Deeps, Ashenvale, the long return from Darnassus — and together they moved out through the Canals and Cathedral Square before striking east. The journey became something almost dreamlike in its sweep: Elwynn's green hush, the heat-shimmer of Burning Steppes, the ash of Searing Gorge, the cold stone of Dun Morogh, all passing beneath his boots in the space of a few quiet minutes. He paused to take pictures as he went — at Elwynn, at the Burning Steppes, at Searing Gorge, at Dun Morogh — small markers laid down like breadcrumbs, the same habit that had followed him across half the continent already.

The road carried them further still, down through Wetlands to Menethil Harbor, out across the Great Sea's grey water to Hillsbrad Foothills and the strand at Southshore, where the Baron's business still waited, unfinished as ever. Rambleon let go of some of it there — Researching the Corruption, Twilight Falls, both abandoned — and took up new threads instead, Crashing the Wickerman Festival and The Power of Pine. A hunter named Quentyn fell in with him for a few minutes near Southshore, brief company on a brief stretch of road, gone again almost before Rambleon had learned the shape of him. He and Cassidy worked through the Azurelode Mine before the road bent back toward Stormwind, and then, astonishingly, bent further still — back east through all the same familiar country, Elwynn to Burning Steppes to Searing Gorge to Dun Morogh again, until at last the Gates of Ironforge opened before him for what felt, after so much walking, like an arrival worth a picture.

Ironforge received them properly this time — the Great Forge, the Military Ward, the Hall of Arms, the full weight of dwarven stone around him. It was there, in the Hall of Arms, that Cassidy parted ways with him, and Rambleon felt the quiet that follows good company leaving, the kind that settles even in a city as busy as this one. Alone again, he tidied his ledger of errands: let Mythology of the Titans and Stepping Stones go, let the old business of Baron's Demise go too, and picked up Stonegear's Search, The Brassbolts Brothers — a crate of crash helmets bound for Wizzle in the Shimmering Flats — and Darkspear Islands Clash. Small commitments, the kind that keep a wandering hunter pointed somewhere. He ended the night in Tinker Town, no further along in level or fortune than when he'd started, but having walked the whole wide circuit of the eastern continent and back, which was its own kind of arrival.
